# Example Article

This note lives in `notes/articles/`, so it's automatically grouped under the
**Articles** section in the sidebar.

## Image support

![A placeholder image](/images/placeholder.png)

Images referenced with `/images/...` are pulled straight from your `images/` folder.

## Nested sections work too

You can nest folders as deep as you like, e.g. `notes/articles/2026/`, and each level
gets its own section in the tree.
